# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. No
harassment, insults, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private information.
Help newcomers, especially people reporting problems with their servers. They may be having a
bad day.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and
may block people who repeatedly do.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled confidentially.
